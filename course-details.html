Visual Communication

Graphic Design & Brand Architecture

Master visual identity systems, modern typography, editorial layout, packaging design, and brand narrative through immersive creative briefs.

Curriculum Overview

This rigorous program trains candidates to operate as high-level visual thinkers capable of defining and executing multifaceted design briefs. You will move through foundational Swiss structural theory into advanced digital systems, type design, responsive identity architectures, and tangible print production.

Modular Syllabus Structure

Anatomy of letterforms, micro-typographic hierarchy, proportional modular grids, contrast, rhythm, and classical editorial layout systems. Students execute poster series and complex typographic monographs.

Visual identity design beyond logos: color spaces, generative brand assets, flexible responsive guidelines, spatial signage, and tangible packaging substrates.

Translating 2D brand identities into interactive web frameworks, kinetic social idents, micro-interactions in After Effects, and Figma design tokens.

12-week self-directed capstone brief under direct mentorship of visiting agency art directors, resulting in a physical publication and digital case study presented at the Annual Degree Showcase.

Tools & Software Covered

Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Figma, Glyphs, Risograph

Learning Outcomes

  • Graduate with 4 in-depth, commercially vetted portfolio case studies.
  • Complete fluency in modern industry software pipelines and print production.
  • Confident verbal presentation and defense during formal design critiques.
