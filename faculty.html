02 / PEDAGOGY HOW WE MENTOR
OUR COMMITMENT

Uncompromising Rigor, Unflinching Support.

At STACKLY, faculty members do not dictate dogmatic answers. Instead, mentors act as creative sounding boards, asking probing questions that force students to examine the fundamental assumptions underpinning their work.

Through weekly studio critiques modeled after top-tier design agencies, students develop the intellectual stamina and technical polish needed to defend their decisions in high-stakes professional presentations.
