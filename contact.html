04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED CAMPUS VISIT & INQUIRY GUIDANCE
VISITOR FAQ

Planning Your Atelier Visit.

Common questions regarding campus walkthroughs, review sessions, and admissions consultations.

Do I need an appointment for a campus tour?

Walk-ins are welcome for general visitor reception during administrative hours (09:00 - 18:00 IST). However, for 1-on-1 portfolio review sessions with senior design faculty, we recommend scheduling 48 hours in advance via the form above.

What should prospective applicants bring along?

Bring any existing sketches, digital portfolios (on tablet or USB), workbooks, or conceptual prototypes. Our admissions mentors will provide detailed, actionable guidance regardless of your current skill level.

Can parents or guardians attend the counseling session?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage parents and guardians to participate in the curriculum overview, tour our specialized laboratories, and review career pathways and placement track records.

How soon will I receive a reply after transmitting?

Our academic admissions desk responds to all direct form submissions and inquiries within 24 business hours with scheduling confirmation and information brochures.
