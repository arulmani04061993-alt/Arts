03 / EVALUATION CRITERIA WHAT THE ADMISSIONS BOARD VALUES
PORTFOLIO CRITERIA

What We Look for in Your Work.

A clear, transparent breakdown of our portfolio assessment framework.

Conceptual Depth (40%)

Why does the project exist? We value your thought process, research, ideation sketches, and how you frame human problems through visual language.

Technical Execution & Form (35%)

Sensibility regarding typography, grid hierarchies, spatial proportion, color harmony, and attention to typographic micro-details.

Curiosity & Voice (25%)

Your unique perspective, self-initiated personal projects, and appetite for experimenting across physical and digital mediums.
