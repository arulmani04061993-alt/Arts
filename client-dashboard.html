Brief Submission Guidelines & Deliverable Checklist

Follow these standardized parameters when framing new briefs to expedite turnaround times and minimize revision cycles.

1. Objective & Audience Scope

Specify targeted user demographics, primary call-to-actions, and emotional tone (e.g. minimalist, brutalist, opulent, academic).

2. Technical Constraints

List target aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), color profiles (DCI-P3, Adobe RGB, CMYK ISO coated), and minimum render DPI requirements.

3. Asset Delivery & Formats

Provide vector logos (.svg / .eps), raw typography licenses, and benchmark reference URLs in Figma or Google Drive.
