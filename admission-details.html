Portfolio Evaluation Rubric

Every applicant's creative dossier is reviewed by two faculty members from the relevant department. We assess submissions across four core pillars:

1. Conceptual Rigor (30%)

Originality of thought, narrative depth, problem framing, and curiosity.

2. Visual Execution (30%)

Compositional balance, typographic hierarchy, color sensitivity, and craftsmanship.

3. Process Documentation (20%)

Sketches, wireframes, early iterations, and rationales for rejected directions.

4. Articulation (20%)

Ability to speak articulately about creative intentions and respond to critique.

Digital Submission Format Guidelines

  • PDF Portfolios: Single document, maximum 25 MB, landscape orientation recommended.
  • Online Portfolios: Direct links to accessible web pages (passwords provided in application notes if private).
  • Motion & Video Reels: Hosted via Vimeo, YouTube Unlisted, or Google Drive (1080p minimum).

Enrollment Stages & Roadmap

Initial screening of transcripts and portfolio dossier by the admissions committee.

Qualified candidates are invited to a 30-minute structured discussion with the department chair.

Successful candidates receive an official admissions letter, scholarship notification, and registration details.

Tuition Structure & Installment Plans

STACKLY operates on an all-inclusive studio fee model. All specialized hardware access, foundry licenses, rendering lab hours, and guest critique series are bundled into your semester fee with zero hidden surcharges.

Undergraduate Diploma (3 Yrs) ₹1,25,000 / Semester
Postgraduate Atelier (2 Yrs) ₹1,60,000 / Semester
Professional Bootcamp (6 Mo) ₹95,000 / One-time
Flexible Payment Options: 0% EMI plans through institutional banking partners, deferred payments, and up to 50% Need-Cum-Merit Fellowships are evaluated during stage 2.
Lateral Entry

Credit Transfer & Prior Learning Assessment

Applicants transferring from accredited domestic or international institutions, or experienced self-taught creatives with a substantial industry track record, may apply for Advanced Standing.

Lateral Entry Up to Year 2

Students with 60+ verified ECTS credits in visual arts or design foundations can directly bypass Tier 1 studios.

Industry Experiential Equivalency

Minimum 2 years of agency or commercial production experience can qualify for core technical module waivers.

Software Certification Credits

Adobe Certified Professional, Figma Expert, or Unreal Authorized Credentials count toward foundational lab credits.
