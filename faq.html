Everything you need to know regarding admissions, academic programs, course durations, studio equipment, and career support.

1. Academic Courses

STACKLY awards professional creative diplomas endorsed by leading design agencies and academic partners. Our credentials prioritize portfolio evaluation, professional competence, and technical mastery over standardized testing.

Due to the intensive studio workload of our core programs, candidates enroll in one primary discipline. However, cross-disciplinary electives in motion design, risograph printing, and UI prototyping are open to all enrolled students.

2. Admissions & Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 18 years old and hold a high school diploma or equivalent secondary credential. A digital portfolio dossier demonstrating creative intent is mandatory for all diploma tracks.

International applicants submit portfolios online and participate in a virtual faculty interview. Visa support documentation is dispatched upon acceptance into on-campus programs.

3. Duration & Learning Formats

Full-time diploma programs run for 9 to 12 months. Specialized certificates (such as Photography and Brand Strategy) run for 6 intensive months. Evening and weekend masterclasses are offered as short 4-to-8 week modules.

Full-time students spend 24 to 30 contact hours per week in studio critiques, technical workshops, and supervised labs, plus 15+ hours of independent self-directed studio execution.

4. Portfolio Development & Careers

Our Career Launchpad integrates portfolio audits, interview rehearsals, client presentation coaching, and direct introductions during our Annual Degree Showcase attended by top agency creative directors.
